Features

For Doctors

Create digital prescriptions with a unique code. Search drugs from a verified catalogue. Track all your patient prescriptions.

For Patients

Retrieve prescriptions with a unique ID. Find the nearest pharmacy with your drugs in stock using our A* algorithm.

For Pharmacists

Register your pharmacy with automatic location detection. Manage your drug inventory and stock levels in real time.

For Admins

Monitor all users, pharmacies, and prescriptions from a central dashboard with live statistics and charts.
